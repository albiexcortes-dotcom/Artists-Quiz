The Artist MB Type Quiz

A quick read on how you work best

Welcome.

This is a Myers-Briggs-style quiz built for stylists. It shows how you naturally work, how you connect with clients, and what helps you do your best work.

It's 28 questions and takes about 6 minutes. There are no right answers and no good or bad types. Pick the option that feels true for you most of the time — not the one you think you should pick.

When you finish, you'll get your 4-letter type and a short "How I Work Best" card you can share with the person who shared this quiz with you.
